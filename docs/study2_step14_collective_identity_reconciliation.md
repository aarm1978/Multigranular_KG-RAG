# Step 14 collective identity reconciliation

The offline consumer reads the v4 item and unit ledgers and the decision-stage v2 authorities. A candidate is the exact sorted set of source-local occurrence IDs in a mechanically admissible strict assessment. Each appearance retains its own unit, field and item index in the pinned v4 ledger. That record supplies the original model text, granularity statement, receipt hash and member-specific source citations. A repeated appearance adds provenance to one proposal.

The consumer reconstructs the v4 permission and reasons for every appearance. Competing collective memberships, held identical memberships, distinct comparisons within their originating window and internal version or variant proposals retain their opposition pointers. The consumer checks route C on the complete group and verifies supporting citation ownership for every member. It retains exact saved A/B safeguard outcomes, checks earlier negative or conflicting pairs, contextual opposition and version/variant relations, and protects all existing mappings and exact human approval memberships. Source capture versions and uncertainty prose retain their original interpretation under the decision-stage policy.

Eligible exact scopes compete with prior overlap-held complete scopes using the existing decision-stage v2 precedence (A, C, B), directly supported complete supersets and equal-support hold rules. The consumer emits a new provisional target only for a selected collective membership. Older competing groups remain under their original decisions. The comparison applies the same policy to the v3-valid units alone and rebuilds their opposition and overlap index. Additional recovered evidence can both introduce proposals and withdraw earlier opportunities.

The ignored package contains the following ledgers.

- `groups.jsonl` records every unique candidate, its appearances, competing scopes, opposition references, route support and disposition. The ordered reference triple identifies unit, item type and original index. Prior pair, assessment and relation references resolve in decision-stage v2.
- `occurrence-mappings.jsonl` includes the prior mappings and each new occurrence-to-provisional-target proposal with its complete original member record. The `materializationHeld` flag preserves the D-16 range gate. Human approvals retain their original restrictions in `historical-approvals.jsonl`.
- `comparison.jsonl` identifies the union of exact proposals selected in either sensitivity run, with separate inclusion flags. The summary counts distinct occurrences per scope and across all scopes. Held and accepted occurrence coverage can overlap across competing proposals.
- The manifest pins the consumed authorities and generated ledgers. All proposals remain subject to the production rule freeze and independent human evaluation.
