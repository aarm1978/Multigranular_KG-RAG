# Step 14 automatic identity acceptance amendment v0.1

Researcher decision, **2026-11-12**. Authority: `study2-step14-automatic-identity-acceptance/0.1.0`. This new, append-only amendment prospectively supersedes **only universal individual human approval before production entity identity acceptance**, including the researcher-only acceptance requirement in the [pilot decision contract](study2_step14_semantic_identity_pilot.md#lossless-occurrence-to-canonical-decision-contract) and corresponding prospective wording in the [granularity policy](study2_step14_identity_granularity_adjudication_v0.1.md). Historical documents remain unchanged and describe the authority in effect at their checkpoints.

A reproducible, frozen automatic procedure may accept entity identity using existing LLM `same_entity_candidate` recommendations, entity-owned identifiers and explicit ontology/referent/granularity/version safeguards. Resulting automatic identities must receive independent, bounded, probabilistic, model-blind human evaluation. **Automatically policy-accepted is not human verified. A positive model recommendation alone is not an existing approval.** This amendment approves that prospective approach, not a production rule, individual identity, or canonical materialization.

The same-scientific-referent criterion, compatible class and supported granularity, preservation of release/implementation/instance specificity, and [scope/completion boundary](study2_step14_scope_and_completion_decision_v0.1.md) remain unchanged. Source roles and namespaces must be established; a locator, common name, architecture, superclass or source capture commit is insufficient. Missing specificity is uncertainty. Complete proposed identity groups must pass safeguards; positive chains cannot bridge missing comparisons, incompatible releases or conflicting referents. Partial pilot subsets never confer approval on a superset or union.

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Researcher-approved | An explicit evidence-bound human decision on its exact membership; the 29 batch01 approvals retain their original authority and scopes. |
| Automatically policy-accepted | A future production decision satisfying a frozen versioned rule and complete-group checks; independently evaluated, never labeled individually human verified. |
| Held | A named safeguard failed or cannot currently be established; no identity action. |
| Rejected/nonidentity | An explicit scoped decision against the proposed identity; do not confuse a model's negative recommendation with such a production decision. |
| Unresolved | Not assessed or outside the supported procedure; no identity action. |

All simulations are separate **provisional** results, not any of these production decisions. Preserve original recommendations and extraction outputs, historical policies, the approval ledger, source assertions, evidence, hashes and occurrence IDs. Future policy decisions require an append-only record of rule/version, exact complete membership, class, referent and granularity/version scope, input/evidence hashes, all safeguard outcomes, authority and supersession links. Preserve conflicting assertions with their provenance. No ontology amendment, canonical allocation, assertion rewriting or graph write follows from this amendment.

## Focused feasibility procedure (provisional, not frozen production)

The [offline consumer](../src/evaluation/step14_automatic_acceptance_preview.py) reads the saved adjudication-triage outputs, their pinned member/endpoint sidecars and existing authority manifests. It does not rerun triage, discovery or model generation. It reuses existing containers and deduplicates only identical complete occurrence sets. No candidate memberships are enlarged; oversized blocks retain unknown overlap without pair expansion.

The unfrozen feasibility interpretation was corrected on 2026-11-12 (`step14-automatic-acceptance-preview/0.2.0`). Two routes supply **predicted** identity support; neither requires a separate semantic-truth certificate:

- **A — exact entity-owned identifier:** an existing complete identifier block, exact scheme/value in a supported class/namespace and own-role declarations for every member, source evidence/provenance, and no demonstrated conflict or unresolved reported ambiguity. This route does not require LLM comparisons: its common own key supplies group-wide predicted support. Local literal declarations, geographic namespaces and request-local links do not qualify.
- **B — LLM-positive semantic identity:** every comparison required for the complete group already has a validated `same_entity_candidate` / entity-level recommendation, evidence IDs bound to both endpoints, selected source evidence, and supplied granularity/version/owner-reference assessments. No model-reported conflict or missing information may remain. These assessments are predictions, not verified truth. The unique existing comparisons must number `n(n−1)/2`; missing comparisons are held, not generated. No similarity threshold or human-label optimization is used.

Both routes require exact class/inventory compatibility. Different explicit granularity values, contradictory single-valued entity-owned identifiers within a supported namespace, negative recommendations and model-reported conflict evidence block provisional acceptance. Multiple own identifiers in one namespace are held as unresolved ambiguity rather than choosing an identifier. Model-reported missing information, abstention, reference-only/conceptual recommendations, unassessed/prior comparisons requiring reconciliation, and missing source evidence also hold affected groups. A negative pair blocks the complete proposed identity without declaring every pair different.

**Missing metadata is distinct from demonstrated incompatibility.** Unspecified structured granularity or absent version metadata is a reported limitation; a supported prediction may still pass. Raw `versions` dictionaries mix entity and source/capture/format metadata: their inequality alone is never a hard scientific version conflict. The consumer does not invent version roles or parse free-text prose into a truth certificate. Scientific referent/version conflicts explicitly surfaced through saved model conflict-evidence fields remain blocking, even when the recommendation is positive. Conflicts expressed only in free text and omitted from those structured conflict/missing-information fields may escape these guards; independent evaluation remains essential. Neither route predicts identical executable artifacts or releases solely from a shared project/resource identifier.

A and B are alternative support routes, deduplicated by identical complete membership. Any provisionally eligible group overlapping a *different* eligible membership is held in full; neither unions nor convenient smaller subsets are produced. Any pilot-approval overlap other than the identical complete approved set remains held. Exact historical approvals are preserved separately, not counted as automatic yield. Oversized groups remain unassessed without expansion.

Outputs report final groups, explicit pairs, distinct occurrences and arithmetic implied-identity-pair counts by support route (A/B), as well as discovery route, class, families and scope. A/B counts may overlap. No new pair rows are synthesized for identifier groups. Pre-overlap eligible counts, overlap-blocked counts, missing explicit comparisons, demonstrated incompatibilities, evidence/ambiguity holds and metadata limitations are separate fields. Per-route preliminary states are not final acceptances after overlap safeguards. Actual yield is determined only by the researcher-terminal run; it is no longer structurally forced to zero. Freeze the final rule and independently evaluate predictions before production; no extra extraction, calls or fallback policy is introduced.

## One-time independent human evaluation blueprint

Freeze the final acceptance rule and its hash, complete disjoint accepted-group frame, evidence packaging, estimands, workload and stopping criteria **before** drawing any cases. This preview draws no sample, emits no evaluation case list and does not use pilot labels for rule optimization. If there are no automatically accepted groups, there is no automatic-identity evaluation population: do not sample held candidates and call it accepted precision.

Use one complete automatically accepted identity group as the primary judgment unit. A correct group means **all** members denote one compatible referent without loss of class, version or granularity specificity. This evaluates group precision, not all-pair precision or discovery recall. Reviewer sees neutral occurrence aliases, complete original source evidence and specificity, but not model outputs, scores, rule route, acceptance reasons, prior recommendations or pilot decisions. A separate preparer retains the sealed mapping. If the reviewer has seen a case before, record that exposure; do not silently replace it. Independent here means human judgments formed without access to model decisions; report any assessor involvement in designing the policy.

For a practical bounded design, freeze the production frame to groups with at most eight members for this evaluation scenario; larger groups remain outside automatic production until a bounded evaluation design for them is approved. This is a proposed evaluation/production-scope restriction, not a hidden filter on the feasibility counts. Stratify accepted groups by acceptance route (A-only, B-only, both), identity scope, and size (2 versus 3–8). These strata expose group-consistency risk. Allocate `n_h` deterministically before drawing, with at least two per noncensus stratum and proportional remainder, capping at `N_h`. If the chosen budget cannot cover these minima, coarsen size then scope deterministically before drawing. Small strata may be censused; if the whole frame is smaller than the budget, census it and report the actual workload.

Draw simple random samples without replacement independently within strata using a recorded seed after policy/frame freeze. Every group has known `pi_g=n_h/N_h`; its design weight is `N_h/n_h`. Estimate overall group precision as `sum_h (N_h/N) * mean_h(correct)`, with finite-population stratified variance `sum_h (N_h/N)^2 * (1-n_h/N_h) * s_h^2/n_h`. Census strata have zero sampling variance. Report uncertainty/design effects and nonresponse; an undetermined judgment is not silently excluded (report conservative incorrect and optimistic correct bounds). Class and exact family-set coverage are prespecified reporting domains, not dozens of underpowered strata. Report their inclusion probabilities and observed coverage; do not replace probability draws to force a class or claim precise small-domain estimates. Any important missed domain or larger-group risk can be illustrated purposively, clearly separated from the weighted precision estimate, with a fixed optional cap of five examples and no adaptive redraws.

One judgment includes checking **every member** and its evidence for whole-group consistency, explicitly looking for a release/unspecified-release bridge, family/instance collapse, subset/base-resource confusion and source/reference-role error. No pairwise majority vote or connected-component approval substitutes for this check. For bounded groups, budget 5–10 minutes per judgment plus 1–2 hours setup/analysis (planning assumptions, not measured):

| Complete-group judgments | Reviewing time | Total with setup | Approx. 95% half-width at p=.95 | Worst-case half-width p=.5 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 60 | 5–10 h | 6–12 h | 5.5 percentage points | 12.7 points |
| 80 | 6.7–13.3 h | 7.7–15.3 h | 4.8 points | 11.0 points |
| 100 | 8.3–16.7 h | 9.3–18.7 h | 4.3 points | 9.8 points |

These planning normal approximations use `1.96 sqrt(p(1-p)/n)`, no finite-population correction and design effect 1. They are not promised precision; unequal weights/clustering can increase uncertainty. Use design-aware intervals for actual stratified results, with a suitable bounded-proportion interval for small/error-free samples rather than reporting zero uncertainty. Secondary occurrence-weighted group correctness may be reported with known group sizes and design weights, explicitly not individual pair precision.

Choose one of 60/80/100 once before drawing. Finish that one sample and at most five separately labeled purposive examples; publish estimates, uncertainty, failures, exposure/nonresponse and limits. The researcher must predeclare an acceptable error criterion before production evaluation. If it fails or precision is inadequate, stop with a documented limitation/held production scope; do not tune on these labels and repeatedly resample until passing. Any substantive policy revision requires a new explicit decision and its evaluation plan, not automatic review rounds. Keep the existing ontology decision gate and separately authorized canonicalization before Step 15.
