**Date:** 2026-10-12  
**Scope:** HydroShare, GitHub, and CIROH Hub semantic production; Publications remains governed by its frozen Step 4 policy.

## 1. Purpose and authority

This policy specifies **admissibility for autonomous KG construction**, not an independent determination of scientific truth. It adopts the same methodological principle as `publication-production-acceptance-policy/0.1.0`: an LLM performs source-constrained semantic interpretation, established validators enforce construction constraints, and admitted source-local assertions may enter the accepted-semantic projection without individual re-adjudication.

The controlling upstream authorities remain Step 11 v0.3, ontology v0.1.6, the accepted Step 12 source-specific request/parser/binding/validator interfaces, and the narrowly approved opt-in `study2-format-conformance/1.0.0` amendment. This policy is a **prospective downstream acceptance authority**; it does not amend historical validator findings, prompt outputs, source snapshots, Steps 1–12, or the frozen Publications extraction/evaluation policy. Full-corpus Publications production follows its own frozen v0.1.5/schema v0.1.3 successor authority. This researcher-approved policy authorizes only its prospective formalization and subsequent bounded offline implementation. Provider calls, full-corpus execution and graph writes require separately authorized steps. The present policy resolves the exploratory acceptance-design criterion in the ChatGPT-side roadmap v13.0 without changing its other milestone boundaries.

## 2. Acceptance rule and disposition

For an independently associated, processable selected response, evaluate the existing replay's `finalRecords` together with each record's `validationRecord`, `findings`, `pendingGates`, bound evidence, resolved endpoints, dependencies, and duplicate indicators. The acceptance layer shall **not** modify those upstream records.

1. **Ordinary inclusion:** include `finalDisposition=validated` when all applicable verifiable requirements and endpoint dependencies hold and the record is not a suppressed duplicate.
2. **Conditional inclusion:** a record with `unresolved_condition` may be included only if **all** remaining reasons are the interpretive conditions explicitly releasable in Section 3, and all verifiable source, evidence, identity, duplicate, endpoint and parent-path requirements hold. A released condition is satisfied **for construction only**; it is not marked as independently semantically verified. Do not inject model-authored or retrospective `gates=True` attestations into the frozen validators.
3. **Dependency closure with complete-reason inspection:** reconsider `unresolved_endpoint` or propagated `unresolved_condition` only when **every** original blocking reason is attributable to a releasable interpretive condition or to an existing local dependency that becomes construction-admitted. Inspect original `parseDiagnostics`, `replayDiagnostics`, `validationRecord.findings` (including simultaneous findings hidden by disposition precedence), `pendingGates`, bound evidence, endpoint references, `dependencies`, duplicate indicators and any required `parentPath`—**never `finalDisposition` alone**. Use the existing exact resolved IDs and paths; require admission of every necessary parent node and relation, and independent evidence for the dependent assertion. Re-evaluate dependencies deterministically without candidate-order dependence. Any separate evidence, source, ontology, identity, endpoint, parent-path, ambiguity or duplicate defect remains blocking. Only the new projection records the resulting acceptance decision; original findings are immutable.
4. **Non-inclusion:** keep `rejected_invalid_assertion`, `failed_source_or_evidence_binding`, `needs_review`, `unresolved_category`, unresolved `deferred`/endpoint records and processing failures out of the autonomous projection. `suppressed_duplicate` is not a second assertion; retain its evidence and references. An abstention produces no assertion. An ordinary `validated` record containing a separate hard failure is not eligible.
5. **No semantic repair:** do not revise model-authored labels, types, relations, quotations, identities or roles; do not downshift a stronger predicate to `mentions`, invent endpoints, or resample merely to increase yield.

## 3. Source-specific conditions

| Family / targets | Interpretive condition releasable for construction | Non-waivable checks |
|---|---|---|
| **HydroShare** `A-D12`, `C-D17` | `explicit_readme_measurement`: LLM interpretation that a specific observation is individuated | Exact verified **README** evidence (not abstract/data files); admitted `Measurement` endpoint; separately bound relation evidence |
| **GitHub** `C-C07` | `repository_specific_purpose_evidence`: LLM assigns the repository's own purpose | `classification=proposed`; one of six frozen `RepositoryPurpose` seeds and exact `approved_category_endpoint`; eligible source prose, bound quote and correct owner; `ambiguous`/`unclassified` remain non-KG |
| **GitHub** `A-C08`, `C-C08`, `A-DOM13`, `C-C20` | Interpretation of `explicit_descriptive_prose` for named `Function`/`Algorithm` and descriptions | Eligible, verified descriptive prose and independently bound node/edge evidence; no source-code/AST interpretation |
| **GitHub** `A-C10`, `C-C09` | Interpretive portion of `own_repository_product` and `explicit_prose_version`: version belongs to the repository's own product | Exact `productRepositoryID`/owner identity; eligible cited prose; `absent_from_deterministic_assertions` checked against trusted accepted deterministic records using **only exact** same-repository version/assertion identity. Suppress proven exact duplicates, retain corroborating citations, and hold the affected candidate if the necessary exact comparison is unavailable; no fuzzy/semantic duplicate resolution |
| **GitHub** `A-P13`, `C-C16` | **None in Step 13:** `explicit_implementation_evidence` does not release `implementsMethod` | Accepted, uniquely bound **Publication** `A-P13 Method` occurrence and Step 14 authorization are mandatory; preserve original unresolved non-KG proposal |
| **CIROH Hub** `A-P13`, `C-DC28` | `supported_method`, `substantive_method_description` | Hub `A-P13 Method` **is model-authorable** and may be a newly admitted **source-local Hub candidate**; `describesMethod` may use that candidate or a trusted exact Method endpoint. Independently bound evidence, valid typing/direction and admitted endpoint required; **no Publication Method prerequisite** |
| **CIROH Hub** `A-DC06`, `C-DC10`, `A-DC08`, `A-DOM12`, `C-DC12`, `C-DC11` | LLM interpretation of substantive `Step`, `Example`, and procedural `Parameter`, including `explanatory_parameter_prose`; `parent_semantic_acceptance_not_evaluated` resolves only when parent becomes construction-admitted | Verified ordered `parentPath`, admitted `Procedure`/`Step` and `hasProcedure`/`hasStep` chain, independently bound parent/dependent evidence; Parameter requires explanatory prose, not heading/code-key alone; visible static MDX only |

Other source-authorized `validated` targets, including `Workflow`, `uses`/`mentions`, `catalogs`, `hasComponent`, `hasProcedure`, `implementedBy` and typed models/tools, use ordinary inclusion. Their **meaning remains governed by the accepted source-specific extraction contracts**, interpreted by the LLM; literal quote binding is not an independent proof of that meaning. A quote may support both a node and a relation if each assertion separately identifies adequate evidence; mere co-occurrence does not establish a stronger role.

## 4. Invariants and coverage

Never waive parser/schema conformity; source-unit eligibility, successful source reading and integrity where the evidence is used; literal evidence binding and provenance for every model-authored node and edge; authorized ontology IDs/signatures; valid endpoint identity and direction; exact parent paths; or duplicate suppression. No abstract fallback entity, model-authored `D-26 mentions`, inactive `DataService/servesDataset`, invented repository-purpose category, name-only cross-source merge, or Step 14 `implementsMethod` resolution is permitted.

Incomplete *other* source units do not invalidate correctly bound assertions from unaffected units. Preserve owner/unit-level incomplete-source diagnostics, unbound citations, excluded or nonselected context, abstentions, unresolved candidates and processing failures. An empty accepted projection is not evidence of an empty scientific source. Full-corpus source coverage and selected-unit boundaries must be separately documented before production.

## 5. Output, provenance and review

Produce a deterministic, hashable, **source-local accepted-semantic projection** containing accepted nodes/edges, exact endpoints and evidence references, and stable links to existing source/request/model/schema/validator/response hashes and this policy ID/version/hash. Preserve all original replay dispositions and rejected/pending diagnostics outside that projection; distinguish ordinary from conditionally admitted records using the existing acceptance-basis conventions, not a new semantic-truth field. Reuse existing controlled-vocabulary seeds and frozen deterministic backbones. No identity consolidation occurs before Step 14.

**Execution and retries.** Reuse the existing Step 12 transport, recording, STOP/authorization and attempt infrastructure; **do not implement a new retry controller**. Allow at most **one initial attempt plus one technical retry** (two attempts total) per request, only when no processable response exists and the original failure is `INVALID_JSON`, `TIMEOUT`, `API_ERROR`, `TRUNCATED_RESPONSE` or `TOKEN_LIMIT` (or an explicitly mapped existing transport equivalent). A retry uses identical source/request content, model, prompt, ontology/schema, selected units, generation settings and limits; preserve both attempts. The **first processable response** ends sampling. No retry for evidence/endpoint binding failures, source-read/integrity failures, semantic holds/rejections, abstentions, sparse output or apparent low quality. If existing transport cannot safely perform the bounded retry, record terminal technical failure rather than building a new subsystem. An execution manifest must pin source coverage, model/request/schema, context/output limits, monetary budget, attempt ceilings and STOP behavior **before any authorized provider call**. Integrate the opt-in format-conformance/schema path only after separately authorized minimal provider compatibility verification. Historical Step 12 authentic outputs and Wave C dispositions remain immutable.

A **small diagnostic audit** must cover both (i) ordinarily `validated` assertions and (ii) conditionally admitted assertions across the three families. **Before full-corpus production,** the researcher must predefine and approve the maximum **number of human judgment operations** for the audit and a separate cap for selective exception review, together with the sampling/selection procedure. Include random sampling within declared strata and separately labeled purposefully difficult cases; no numerical budget is presumed here. Neither audit nor exception review is a mandatory per-assertion certification, second annotation campaign, gold construction or population-precision estimate. Known Wave C examples may inform diagnostic categories, but may not be used to retune prompts, modify historical Wave C outputs or retrospectively correct accepted evaluations. Critical systematic findings trigger a scoped hold/investigation and, if justified, a prospective versioned amendment.

## 6. Minimal implementation and acceptance gate

Implement only (a) a post-replay disposition/condition/dependency adapter, (b) the source-local projection and existing-provenance references, and (c) a **narrow exact** deterministic-assertion duplicate check for GitHub `ModelVersion` if not already covered by trusted accepted records. Reuse the existing family request/reader/parser/binder/validators, GitHub purpose seeds, immutable replay reports, and Publications projection conventions. No semantic classifier, LLM judge, confidence system, new annotation UI, new ontology, or generalized entity-resolution subsystem.

**Step 13.0 formalization:** retain this researcher-approved prospective version as a tracked repository contract, pinned by commit and file hash. **Implementation acceptance** is separate: run only focused T1/T2 tests for ordinary inclusion, released interpretation, complete-reason dependency closure (including simultaneous non-releasable failures), Hub-local `Method`, blocked GitHub `implementsMethod`, exact deterministic `ModelVersion` duplicates, evidence/source failures, immutability and projection reproducibility. Update the operational handoff at the implementation milestone. No full test suite, provider/model calls, replay of authentic Wave C for tuning, corpus-wide audit, or graph write is part of this implementation task. Provider compatibility checks and full-corpus runs require explicit later authorization plus the predefined audit/review budgets and execution manifest. **Step 14A remains optional/default SKIPPED.**
